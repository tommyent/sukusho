# Sukusho 1.3.0 beta 1

The first beta of Sukusho 1.3.0: clipboard history, image optimizing, a new recording bar, and Sukusho on macOS 26. Open the DMG and drag Sukusho to Applications.

Tested on macOS 26.5.1 and 27.2.1.

**Getting later betas:** 1.2.0 has no beta switch, so this beta comes only as a download. Once it's installed, turn on **Settings › About › Get beta updates** to be offered the next betas. With the switch off, Sukusho offers only regular releases.

## New since 1.2.0

### Clipboard history and the Library
- **Clipboard history** keeps what you copy: text, links, colors, images and files. It's off until you turn it on in **Settings › Clipboard**, which walks you through setup and lets you choose apps whose copies are ignored. Copies that apps mark as private or temporary are never kept.
- **⌃⌘V** opens a popup to search your history and paste. **⌘1** to **⌘0** paste your saved clips, and **Actions** (⌘K) has the rest. Clipboard History is also in the menu bar menu.
- The Library has a **Clipboard** section, with **History** and **Snippets**, in a grid or a list. Images get a title and keywords, read on your Mac, and can be opened in the editor, optimized or converted.
- When another app, or Sukusho's own Optimize, puts a smaller copy of an image back on the clipboard, it becomes the newest version of the same card, and the original is kept beside it.
- **Save (★)** keeps a capture or a clip in **Library › Saved**, out of reach of the history's clean-up. Captures you named, tagged or shared before are saved for you.
- Captures are titled by app and window, and Quick Look follows your selection.
- Settings has new **Shortcuts**, **Library** and **Clipboard** panes. **Show Library** has a shortcut that works anywhere.

### Screenshots and the editor
- **Area screenshots** use Sukusho's own picker: hold **Shift** to snap to a box on screen, or **Option** for a loupe.
- Preview cards close after 10 seconds by default, overlap instead of piling up, and stay while the pointer is over any of them.
- **Text** is sized by its handles, with a size badge and snapping. New text gets a light halo so it reads over busy screenshots, and **⌘⌥+** and **⌘⌥−** resize it.
- Tool options sit in one place at the top of the inspector, under the tool's name. **Crop** takes over the inspector while you crop.
- **Numbered circles** can have a short tail. The magnifier comes in circle, rectangle and rounded-square shapes.
- A drawing tool stays selected after each annotation; **Escape** returns to Select.
- A **Transparent** background keeps padding, corners and shadow.
- **Scrolling Capture** shows a live preview strip, **Space** starts and pauses auto-scroll, and pinned sidebars and window edges stitch more cleanly.

### Recording bar and Studio
- The recording bar has a new look: what's on is lit, the countdown shows in the bar, red means recording and amber means paused. The microphone and system-audio chips show their levels during a take.
- **Area recordings** wait for **Record**, so you can move and resize the area first.
- Recordings start with no background, as screenshots do, and are captured at even sizes.
- Studio's inspector works like the image editor's: sections are switches or always open, and what isn't available says what unlocks it.
- Export doesn't offer **Include audio** for a recording with no sound.
- Videos are saved to **~/Movies/Sukusho**; images stay in Pictures.

### Capture Text
- Lines the screen wrapped are joined into paragraphs, with hyphens kept. Turn on **Settings › Screenshots › Keep line breaks in Capture Text** to keep every line.
- Tables and lists keep their shape, and text captured on a non-Retina screen is read at twice its size.
- Capture Text uses Sukusho's area picker, and says what it copied.

### Image optimizing and the shelf
- **Optimize** makes screenshots smaller without changing a pixel, using oxipng, libwebp and MozJPEG, included in the app. **Convert to WebP…** and **Convert to JPEG…** make a smaller copy in another format.
- In **Settings › Optimize**, new captures are optimized in the background, copies are optimized as they're made, and shares to Cloud are optimized before upload. **⇧⌘C** copies the original.
- The copy made right after a capture doesn't wait: the original goes on the clipboard at once, and the smaller copy takes its place when it's ready.
- **The shelf:** shake the pointer while dragging files from Finder or the Desktop, then drop them on the shelf to optimize, convert or share them.

### Privacy of Sukusho's windows
- **Settings › General › Include Sukusho windows in captures** decides whether preview cards, the recording bar, editors and Studio appear in Sukusho's own screenshots and recordings. Other apps and screen sharing see them either way.
- The clipboard popup is always left out of Sukusho's own captures, including a recording that's already running, and Sukusho asks macOS to hide it from other apps too.
- Pinned screenshots follow the same setting.

### Updates
- **Settings › About › Get beta updates** offers betas as well as every regular release.

### macOS 26
- Sukusho now opens on macOS 26. A notice at launch says some features may be missing there; **Don't show again** hides it for that macOS version.
