# Sukusho 1.3.0

Clipboard history, themes with a new card look called Chip, image optimizing, a new recording bar with markers for bug reports, captures you can drag out of the Library, and Sukusho on macOS 26. Open the DMG and drag Sukusho to Applications.

Tested on macOS 26.5.1 and 27.3.

**Getting it:** Sukusho 1.2.0 offers this update by itself, or choose **Sukusho › Check for Updates…**. If you ran a 1.3.0 beta, you're offered it too. **Settings › About › Get beta updates** decides whether you're also offered later betas.

## Scrolling Capture is a beta

Scrolling Capture is marked **Beta** in the Capture menu, the menu bar menu and **Settings › Shortcuts**. It works for many pages, with known limits: auto-scroll may not stop at the end of a page, sticky sidebars and scrolling areas inside a page can stitch poorly, and scrolling with **Page Down** captures nothing, so scroll with the trackpad or mouse.

## New since 1.2.0

### Themes and Chip
- **Settings › Appearance** is a new pane. **Mode** chooses System, Light or Dark. **Light theme** and **Dark theme** each choose a look for that appearance. The **Workspace** background (Dots, Plain or a wallpaper, with Dim and Blur) moved here from General.
- **Chip** is a new look for Library cards, in Light and Dark: a square, flat card with the picture set in from the top and sides, like a paint chip, a fine line under the picture, and a label strip with a subtle paper grain. It keeps Classic's colors and drop shadow. Pick it in **Settings › Appearance**.
- **Classic**, the look of the betas, stays the default. Library cards look like heavy card stock, with a lit top edge and a thin lip, and lift a little under the pointer.

### Clipboard history and the Library
- **Clipboard history** keeps what you copy: text, links, colors, images and files. It's off until you turn it on in **Settings › Clipboard**, which walks you through setup and lets you choose apps whose copies are ignored. Copies that apps mark as private or temporary are never kept.
- **⌃⌘V** opens a popup to search your history and paste. **⌘1** to **⌘0** paste the first ten clips shown, and **Actions** (⌘K) has the rest.
- The Library has a **Clipboard** section, with **History** and **Snippets**, in a grid or a list. Images get a title and keywords, read on your Mac.
- **Move to Saved** (or the gray **☆** on a card) keeps a capture or clip in **Library › Saved**, where clipboard history's clean-up never removes it. **⌘Z** undoes it.
- Clipboard, Snippets and Saved select, copy and preview like Captures: **⌘-click**, **Shift-click**, drag a rectangle or **⌘A**; **⌘C** copies every selected item; **Quick Look** (Space) follows the selection. **Control-click** opens a card's menu.
- **Drag captures out:** drag a screenshot or recording from the Library or Saved to Finder or another app, one or several at a time. A renamed capture uses its Library name, and an edited recording arrives with its edits.
- Captures are titled by app and window, and each card shows the icon of the app it came from.

### Screenshots and the editor
- **Area screenshots** use Sukusho's own picker: hold **Shift** to snap to a box on screen, or **Option** for a loupe.
- Preview cards close after 10 seconds by default, overlap instead of piling up, and stay while the pointer is over them.
- **Text** annotations are sized by their handles, get a light halo so they read over busy screenshots, and check spelling: Control-click an underlined word to choose a correction.
- Tool options sit at the top of the inspector. **Numbered circles** can have a short tail, the magnifier comes in three shapes, and a **Transparent** background keeps padding, corners and shadow.
- **⌘C** copies at once, and a copy never lands on something you copied after it.

### Recording, Studio and bug reports
- The recording bar has a new look: the countdown shows in the bar, red means recording and amber means paused, and the microphone and system-audio chips show their levels.
- **Area recordings** wait for **Record**, so you can move and resize the area first.
- **Mark a bug while recording:** press **⌃⇧M** in a bug report to place numbered markers, circle a detail with **Oval** or draw with **Pen**. The Agent Prompt lists each marker's number, time, position and still image.
- Videos are saved to **~/Movies/Sukusho**, or a folder you choose in **Settings › General**.

### Capture Text
- Lines the screen wrapped are joined into paragraphs. Turn on **Settings › Screenshots › Keep line breaks in Capture Text** to keep every line.
- Tables and lists keep their shape, and text on a non-Retina screen is read at twice its size.

### Image optimizing and the shelf
- **Optimize** makes screenshots smaller without changing a pixel, using oxipng, libwebp and MozJPEG, included in the app. **Convert to WebP…** and **Convert to JPEG…** make a smaller copy in another format.
- **Settings › Optimize** can optimize new captures in the background, copies as they're made, and shares to Cloud before upload. **⇧⌘C** copies the original.
- **The shelf:** shake the pointer while dragging files from Finder, then drop them on the shelf to optimize, convert or share them.

### Shortcuts
- **Settings › Shortcuts** lists every shortcut in order. New defaults: **Screen Recording** is **⇧⌘5** and **Capture Text** **⇧⌘O**; shortcuts you set yourself stay as they are.
- **⇧⌘3**, **⇧⌘4** and **⇧⌘5** are also macOS's screenshot shortcuts. Sukusho says so, and offers **Use Other Keys** or **Open Keyboard Shortcuts…** to turn macOS's off.
- A shortcut can be any one modifier with a key, **F1** to **F20** on its own, or a modifier key tapped once or twice, such as **Right ⌥** or **⌘ ⌘**.

### Privacy of Sukusho's windows
- **Settings › General › Include Sukusho windows in captures** decides whether preview cards, the recording bar, editors and Studio appear in Sukusho's own screenshots and recordings.
- The clipboard popup is always left out of Sukusho's own captures, and Sukusho asks macOS to hide it from other apps too.

### Updates and macOS 26
- **Settings › About › Get beta updates** offers betas as well as every regular release. It's off in a regular release, and your own choice stays.
- Sukusho now opens on macOS 26. A notice at launch says some features may be missing there; **Don't show again** hides it for that macOS version.

## Fixes
- Screenshots say so when Sukusho isn't allowed to record the screen.
- The area picker always shows its crosshair, even when another app is in front.
- A new capture's text becomes searchable right away, without waiting for Apple Intelligence to title another capture.
- The notices at launch no longer hold up clipboard history or pull Sukusho in front of other apps.
