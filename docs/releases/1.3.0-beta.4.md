# Sukusho 1.3.0 beta 4

The fourth beta of Sukusho 1.3.0: **⌘C** copies at once, a copy never lands on something you copied after it, Control-click opens the Library's menus, the area picker always shows its crosshair, and a new capture's text no longer waits to become searchable. Open the DMG and drag Sukusho to Applications.

Tested on macOS 26.5.1 and 27.2.1.

**Getting this beta:** with beta 1, 2 or 3 installed and **Settings › About › Get beta updates** on (it starts on in a beta), Sukusho offers beta 4 by itself. From 1.2.0, download it here.

## New since beta 3

### ⌘C copies at once
- With **Settings › Optimize › Optimize when copying** on, **⌘C** in the editor used to wait until the smaller image was made, up to a few seconds for a large screenshot, before anything reached the clipboard. Now the image goes on the clipboard as soon as you press **⌘C**, and the editor closes. The smaller image, with the same pixels, takes its place when it's ready, while the clipboard still holds it. A paste in between gets the image as it is.
- **Copy** on one capture in the Library and on a preview card, and the copy right after a capture, work the same way.
- Clipboard history keeps one entry for the copy. It shows the smaller image, with the original kept as its earlier version.
- Pressing **⌘C** again on the same image adds nothing, and doesn't make a second smaller copy.
- **Copy Original** (**⇧⌘C**), and every copy with **Optimize when copying** off, copy the image as it is, as before.

### A copy never lands on something newer
- If you copy something else while Sukusho is still preparing a copy you asked for, here or in another app, your later copy stays on the clipboard, and Sukusho doesn't say Copied. This holds for **⌘C** in the editor while it saves your edits, for copying one or several items in the Library, and for **Copy** on a preview card or a pinned screenshot.

## Fixes
- **Control-click** on a Library card or list row opens its menu, as a right-click does, on every page. With several cards selected, the selection stays. Control-click on a card's **☆** opens the menu instead of moving the capture to Saved. In a rename field, Control-click opens the text menu, and on a Clipboard section title it opens the page's menu.
- The area picker for **Area Screenshot**, **Capture Text** and **Video Area** sometimes showed the arrow instead of its bold crosshair when another app was in front. It now shows the crosshair from the moment it appears.
- A new capture's text could wait to become searchable while Apple Intelligence was still titling another capture. Now text is read as captures arrive.
  - A title that takes more than a minute is stopped. Titles that don't come through are tried again after five minutes, then less often, up to once an hour.
  - Turning **Use Apple Intelligence for titles and tag suggestions** back on, or starting **Title and tag existing captures**, tries again at once.
- When text recognition fails on an image, Sukusho tries its faster recognition before giving up. If neither can read the image, **Copy Text from Image** and **Capture Text** say "Text recognition failed" and leave the clipboard as it was. Shortcuts and agents get an error too.
