# Sukusho 1.3.0 beta 3

The third beta of Sukusho 1.3.0: Library cards look like heavy card stock, with roomier and larger captions; clipboard text sits on a cool gray well with a plain kind symbol; and the notices at launch no longer hold anything up or pull Sukusho to the front. Open the DMG and drag Sukusho to Applications.

Tested on macOS 26.5.1 and 27.2.1.

**Getting this beta:** with beta 1 or beta 2 installed and **Settings › About › Get beta updates** on (it starts on in a beta), Sukusho offers beta 3 by itself. From 1.2.0, download it here.

## New since beta 2

### Library cards
- Cards look like heavy card stock, with a lit top edge and a thin lip underneath. A card lifts a little under the pointer and sinks while you press it, and a selected card's lip takes the accent color along with its ring. This is on every page with cards: Captures, Clipboard, Snippets, Saved and Cloud.
- The picture sits in a soft, shaded well.
- The card's edges, padding and corners grow with the size slider, so large cards keep their proportions.
- The caption has more room above it and at the sides, and its title and date are a point larger. Narrow cards show the time alone.
- The gray **☆**, the orange draft dot and the Agent Prompt mark in the caption are raised like the app icon beside them.

### Clipboard
- Text, code, link, snippet and file cards sit on a cool gray well instead of the picture well. Images and colors keep the picture well.
- Text, code and link clips are marked with a plain gray symbol (**Aa**, **</>** and a link) in the card's bottom-right corner, without the pill behind it. List rows and the clipboard popup use the same symbols. On cards, VoiceOver still says text, code or link.

## Fixes
- The notice about macOS's screenshot shortcuts held up clipboard history until you answered it. Now copies keep being recorded while it's open. **Return** gives the highlighted answer and **Escape** is **Not Now**. Closing it without an answer still brings it back at the next launch.
- The notice that Sukusho hasn't been tested on your version of macOS opened off to one side and went behind the Library. It now opens in the middle of the screen and stays in front of Sukusho's windows.
- The notices wait until Sukusho is in front. If you switch to another app right after opening Sukusho, Sukusho stays behind, and the notices show when you come back to it. While another app is in front, an open notice steps aside and comes back with Sukusho.
- When both notices show at once, the second opens a little lower and to the right, so both can be seen.
